# Central

> **Status:** stub — nothing in this repository references Central.
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-09

## What is known

Nothing, from here. `index.html` and `voice-v2.html` know exactly one back-end
URL — the Gateway — and no second system. Central may sit behind the Gateway,
beside it, or above it; the browser cannot tell.

## What belongs on this page

* What Central is and what it owns that Panchita Personal does not.
* Its relationship to the Gateway: is Central behind it, or is the Gateway a
  component of Central?
* Where it runs, and how it is reached.
* Who or what talks to it besides Panchita.

## Open questions

* Does Central own identity and memory, with Panchita Personal as a thin
  client? The blueprint hints at it but nothing states it.
* Does Central serve the business modules (03) directly?
* Is Central live, or is it aspirational and mis-filed under Live Systems?

Until the first question is answered, the
[Architecture Map](../00-master-blueprint/architecture-map.md) cannot draw an
edge to Central.
