# Business

> **Status:** concept — named in the Master Blueprint. **The name is broad
> enough that nothing should be built against it.**
> **Lifecycle:** CONCEPT ONLY
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-10

## What is known

Approved as a business domain. Nothing else.

## The problem with the name

Every other domain in this section names something specific: a trade, a
function, a market. "Business" names the category they all belong to.

Three readings, all plausible:

1. **General business operations** — the admin that is not truck repair, real
   estate or recruiting: entity paperwork, licences, insurance, compliance.
2. **A business the owner runs** that is not yet separately named.
3. **Business intelligence** — in which case it overlaps
   [Analytics](../10-platform-components/analytics.md) and belongs elsewhere.

## Why this matters more than it looks

A domain that means "everything else" attracts work that belongs somewhere
specific, and then nobody can find it. If reading (1) is right, this page should
be renamed to what it actually covers — *Operations*, *Admin*, *Compliance* —
rather than kept as a catch-all.

**One sentence from the owner resolves it.** Until then the page holds the
question.

## Deliberately undefined

Everything, including whether this is one domain or a bucket that should be
split.
