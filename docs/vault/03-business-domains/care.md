# Care

> **Status:** concept — named in the Master Blueprint. No definition given.
> **Lifecycle:** CONCEPT ONLY
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-10

## What is known

Approved as a business domain. Nothing about what is cared for is decided.

## The readings

* **Customer care** — support, follow-up, complaint handling, after-service.
  Nearest to the existing businesses, and closest to
  [Sales](sales.md) and [Marketing](marketing.md).
* **Employee care** — the people side: onboarding, wellbeing, retention. Would
  sit next to [Recruiter](recruiter.md), which handles getting them in the door.
* **A care business** — an actual services venture, unrelated to the others.

## If it is customer care, one thing already exists

The shop's social-content skill covers **asking customers for reviews**. That is
a slice of after-service contact done today, and it is currently filed under
[Marketing](marketing.md). Whether review requests are marketing or care is
exactly the boundary this page would settle.

## The constraint that applies to any reading

Customer care and employee care both mean holding data about **people who are
not the owner** — the same property that gates
[Recruiter](recruiter.md). That makes
[04 · Permissions](../04-security/permissions.md) and
[04 · Audit](../04-security/audit.md) prerequisites, and both are concepts
today.

## Deliberately undefined

Who is cared for, by whom, and through what channel.
