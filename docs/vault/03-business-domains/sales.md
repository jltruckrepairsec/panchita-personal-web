# Sales

> **Status:** concept — named in the Master Blueprint. No definition given.
> **Lifecycle:** CONCEPT ONLY
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-10

## What is known

Approved as a business domain. Which businesses it serves is undecided.

## Selling already happens in two places

Neither is filed here, and that is the tension this page has to resolve:

* **The shop** quotes work. Estimates and invoices for J & L Truck Repair are
  covered by an existing skill, and unclosed quotes are already a tracked
  metric — see [Truck Repair](truck-repair.md).
* **Wholesaling** is almost entirely sales: motivated sellers, cold calling and
  texting, offers, negotiation, cash buyers, dispositions. See
  [Real Estate](real-estate.md).

So the question is not "should there be sales?" but **"is Sales a domain of its
own, or the sales function inside each domain?"** Those produce different
systems: one pipeline shared across businesses, or a pipeline per business with
nothing in common.

## The distinction worth keeping

* [Marketing](marketing.md) — creating interest
* **Sales** — converting it
* [Care](care.md) — keeping it after conversion

Whether that split earns three separate domains for a business this size is
genuinely open, and worth answering before any of them is built.

## Deliberately undefined

Which businesses, what a pipeline holds, and whether Panchita ever contacts a
prospect directly — which would be an action taken on the owner's behalf and so
falls under [04 · Guardian](../04-security/guardian.md).
