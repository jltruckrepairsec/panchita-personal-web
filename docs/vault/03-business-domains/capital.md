## What is known

Approved as a business domain. Nothing about its scope is decided.

## Capital and Financial

[Financial](financial.md) is money *flowing* — cash position, receivables,
payroll, margin, tax set-aside. Capital is plausibly money *funding* — raising
it, deploying it, and what it is deployed into.

For a wholesaling business that distinction is concrete: Financial tracks
whether this month works; Capital decides whether to fund a deal. But the split
is inference, and the two would be built as one system if nobody separates them
deliberately.

## Not Panchita Coin

[12 · Panchita Coin / Economy](../12-governance-and-legacy/panchita-coin-economy.md)
is a long-term concept explicitly capped as *not a project, not a financial
product*. **Capital is a different thing entirely**: real money in real
businesses. The two should never be conflated, in this vault or anywhere else.

## The constraint that gates this page

Capital is the second domain after [Financial](financial.md) that touches money
directly, and the same gate applies, harder:

* [P6 · human approval for critical actions](../00-master-blueprint/principles.md#p6--human-approval-for-critical-actions) —
  not implemented
* [04 · Permissions](../04-security/permissions.md) — no model exists
* [04 · Audit](../04-security/audit.md) — no trail exists

**Read-only reporting and moving money are different capabilities wearing the
same name.** Conflating them is the risk on this page, exactly as it is on
Financial.

## Deliberately undefined

Whose capital, what it funds, and whether Panchita ever does more than report on
it.
