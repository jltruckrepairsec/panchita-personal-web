# Panchita Coin / Economy

> **Status:** **LONG-TERM CONCEPT ONLY.** Named in the Master Blueprint and
> deliberately constrained by owner decision on 2026-09-10.
> **Lifecycle:** CONCEPT ONLY — long-term
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-10

## Read this before mentioning this concept anywhere

By owner decision, Panchita Coin / economy:

* is **not an active project**
* is **not a commitment**
* is **not a token launch**
* is **not a financial product**
* is **not a current roadmap priority**

> **It only becomes relevant after real adoption and clear utility.**

Neither of those conditions is close. Panchita has one user, one production
artefact, and a business-domain map where nothing is built. Adoption is not a
number anyone is tracking, and no utility a coin would serve has been
identified.

## Why the constraint is written this strongly

Every other concept page in sections 10–12 is a parking space, and being
mentioned in a document carries no weight. **This one is different**, because
the words "coin", "token" and "economy" carry implications that a page in a
knowledge vault cannot control:

* They read as a financial offering whether or not that is meant.
* They attract attention and expectation disproportionate to a line in a map.
* Repeated in a roadmap, a deck or a conversation, they become a commitment
  nobody intended to make.

So this page exists to **hold the concept and cap it** — to record that it was
named, and to make its status unmistakable to anyone who finds it later,
including anyone quoting the vault.

## What is actually recorded

That the concept exists in the owner's long-term thinking. That is the whole of
it. No mechanism, no purpose, no unit, no participants, no timeline.

## Deliberately undefined

Everything. Nothing about this concept should be designed, specified, estimated
or scheduled, and the vault should not develop this page further until the two
conditions above are met.

## The precondition worth naming

If a Panchita economy ever means anything, it means value moving between
parties. That requires
[04 · Permissions](../04-security/permissions.md),
[04 · Audit](../04-security/audit.md) and
[04 · Guardian](../04-security/guardian.md) to be real — and today all three are
concepts. Even the earliest sensible version of this idea sits behind work that
has not started.
