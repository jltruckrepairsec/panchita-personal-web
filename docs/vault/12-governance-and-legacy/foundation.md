## What is known

Approved as a governance/long-horizon concept. Nothing else.

## The ambiguity

"Foundation" has two readings that share no work at all:

1. **A legal or organizational entity** — a foundation in the institutional
   sense, which would sit naturally beside [Legacy](legacy.md) and
   [Succession](succession.md) as the vehicle by which Panchita outlives its
   owner.
2. **A technical foundation** — the base layer everything is built on, which
   would overlap heavily with
   [Panchita Core](../10-platform-components/panchita-core.md).

Reading (1) belongs in this section. Reading (2) belongs in
[10](../10-platform-components/README.md) and would make this page a duplicate
of Core.

Its placement here follows the owner's own grouping, which put Foundation in the
governance family — so reading (1) is the better guess. **A guess is not a
decision**, and this page records the question rather than closing it.

## Deliberately undefined

Everything, including which section this page ultimately belongs in.
