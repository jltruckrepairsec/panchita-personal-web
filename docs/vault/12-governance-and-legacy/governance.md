# Governance

> **Status:** concept — named in the Master Blueprint. No definition given.
> **Lifecycle:** CONCEPT ONLY
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-10

## What is known

Approved as a governance/long-horizon concept. Nothing about its form is
decided.

## Where it sits among the three governing concepts

* **Governance** — *who decides*, and how decisions are made, recorded and
  changed.
* [**Standards**](../10-platform-components/standards.md) — *the rules the
  system follows*.
* [**Guardian**](../04-security/guardian.md) — *what is enforced at runtime*.

A standard without governance is a suggestion; governance without enforcement is
paperwork.

## What already behaves like governance

More than one might expect, all of it informal and all of it Luis:

* **Owner decisions** are already the mechanism by which this vault changes.
  Every page in sections 10–12 exists because of one, dated 2026-09-10, and the
  vault records which decision produced what.
* [**Architecture Decisions**](../07-change-history/architecture-decisions.md)
  records six technical decisions with their consequences — including one marked
  *under review* rather than settled, which is a governance behaviour: refusing
  to record a decision while its subject is still failing.
* [**The Constitution**](../00-master-blueprint/constitution.md) and
  [**Core Principles**](../00-master-blueprint/principles.md) are the rules
  those decisions are made against.

So Panchita has governance today: one decision-maker, decisions written down,
and rules to decide against. What it does not have is any of that surviving him
— which is exactly [Legacy](legacy.md) and [Succession](succession.md).

## Deliberately undefined

Who decides besides the owner, how a decision is proposed or appealed, what
requires approval versus notification, and how governance changes itself.
