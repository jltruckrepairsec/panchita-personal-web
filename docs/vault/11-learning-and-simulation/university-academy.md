## What belongs here

The name most plausibly means training — turning what the owner knows into
something that teaches, whether the learner is a new mechanic, a virtual
assistant, or Panchita herself. That is inference from the name; nothing
confirms it.

If that reading holds:

* Who learns: staff, contractors, or the system.
* What is taught, and where the source material comes from — likely the SOPs in
  [05](../05-knowledge/sops.md).
* Whether it produces material (courses, checklists) or delivers it
  interactively.
* How completion is tracked, if at all.

## Its likely real relationship to the rest of the vault

If University teaches from SOPs, then [05 · SOPs](../05-knowledge/sops.md) is
its input and this is a consumer of the knowledge section rather than a
producer. That would make writing the SOPs a prerequisite, and would explain why
it has no code yet.

It also sits next to [Mentor](../10-platform-components/mentor.md), and the two
need keeping apart: the plausible split is that University teaches a curriculum
away from the work, while Mentor guides someone during it. That is inference and
should be confirmed.
