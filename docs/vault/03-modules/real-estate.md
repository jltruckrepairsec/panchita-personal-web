## The business

J & J Real Development Inc — wholesaling.

## What the existing skill covers

A wholesaling playbook exists as an assistant skill outside this repository,
covering: motivated sellers, pulling lead lists, cold calling and texting,
comps, ARV / MAO / assignment-fee arithmetic, making and negotiating offers,
purchase and assignment contracts, title companies, cash buyers, dispositions,
daily and weekly KPIs, and seller scripts in English and Spanish.

That is a complete operational playbook. What it is not is a module: it
describes how the work is done, not how Panchita participates in it.
