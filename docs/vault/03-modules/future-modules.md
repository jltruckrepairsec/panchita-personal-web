## Purpose

Where a module idea waits before it earns a page of its own. Nothing here is
committed to.

## Nothing is listed yet

No candidate modules are recorded. This is not an oversight — it is the honest
state, and it is better than a list of names nobody has thought about.

## The bar for promotion

Before an idea here becomes a page in this section, it should have:

1. A business outcome it produces, stated in one sentence.
2. A named source of truth for the data it needs.
3. Somebody who would use it weekly.

An idea that cannot clear all three is an idea, not a module.

## Where ideas should go instead

Anything with a timeline belongs in [09 — Backlog](../09-backlog/) —
`Later` for someday, `Next` for the coming cycle. This page is for shapeless
ideas that do not yet have enough form to be scheduled.
