## What exists today

A social-content skill for J & L Truck Repair specifically: posts and review
requests for the shop. It is explicitly scoped to the shop and does not apply
to the owner's other businesses.

So marketing exists as one narrow capability for one business, not as a module.

## What belongs here

* Which businesses have a marketing need: the shop, J & J Real Development, or
  both.
* Channels: which platforms, which accounts, who posts.
* Whether Panchita drafts only, or publishes.
* Brand voice per business — they are different audiences and should not share
  one tone.
* Review generation, which is currently the highest-value slice.
