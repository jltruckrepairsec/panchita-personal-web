## What is known

## The distinction this page has to hold

Mentor sits close to two other approved concepts, and the three would collapse
into one another if nobody keeps them apart:

| Concept | Plausible distinction |
| --- | --- |
| **Mentor** (platform) | Guides a *person* while they work — in the moment, in context |
| [University / Academy](../11-learning-and-simulation/university-academy.md) (learning) | Teaches a curriculum, away from the work |
| [Guardian](../04-security/guardian.md) (security) | Constrains what may be *done*, regardless of who is doing it |

That table is inference, not owner decision. It is written down so the
distinction is at least visible; it should be confirmed or replaced.

## Deliberately undefined

* Who is mentored — Luis, employees, customers, or Panchita herself.
* Whether Mentor is a component, a mode, or a role another component plays.
* What it draws on. If it teaches from [05 · SOPs](../05-knowledge/sops.md),
  then those have to exist first — and today they do not.
