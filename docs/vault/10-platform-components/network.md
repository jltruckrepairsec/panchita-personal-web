# Network

> **Status:** concept — named in the Master Blueprint. No definition given, and
> the name is ambiguous enough that nothing should be built against it.
> **Lifecycle:** CONCEPT ONLY
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-10

## What is known

Approved as a platform component. Nothing else.

## The ambiguity

"Network" carries at least three unrelated meanings in a system like this, and
they have nothing in common technically:

1. **A network of people** — customers, vendors, cash buyers, mechanics,
   referrals. Close to
   [Marketplace](../03-business-domains/marketplace.md) and to the cash-buyer
   lists in [Real Estate](../03-business-domains/real-estate.md).
2. **A network of Panchita instances** — multiple deployments that share
   knowledge or capability. Close to
   [Legacy](../12-governance-and-legacy/legacy.md) and
   [Foundation](../12-governance-and-legacy/foundation.md).
3. **Infrastructure networking** — connectivity, boundaries, transport. Close to
   [P3 · zero trust](../00-master-blueprint/principles.md#p3--security--zero-trust).

Reading (1) makes this a business capability that is mis-filed here. Reading (2)
makes it a long-horizon governance concern. Reading (3) makes it security.

**One sentence from the owner picks one and discards two.** Until then this page
holds the ambiguity rather than resolving it by guessing — the vault's job is to
make the open question visible.
